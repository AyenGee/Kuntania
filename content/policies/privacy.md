# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

We respect your privacy and handle your personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

## 1. Who is responsible

The responsible party is Beene Milambo trading as Kunta Nia, a sole proprietor, of 46 Nielson Drive, Blairgowrie, 2194, Johannesburg. Our Information Officer is Beene Milambo, reachable at kuntania.contact@gmail.com or on 062 176 2631.

## 2. What we collect and why

- **Orders:** name, email, phone number, delivery address, and order details. We use these to process payment, deliver your order, send order updates, keep tax and accounting records, and handle returns.
- **Contact messages:** name, email, optional phone number and your message. We use these to reply to you.
- **Newsletter:** your email address and the time you agreed. We use this only to send you marketing emails you have opted in to. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link.

We only collect what we need. We do not create customer accounts, and we do not store your card details; payments are handled by Yoco.

## 3. Mandatory and voluntary information

Providing your name, contact details, and delivery address is mandatory to process and deliver your order; without this information we cannot fulfil your purchase. Subscribing to our newsletter is voluntary.

## 4. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who help us run the shop, and only as needed:

- **Yoco:** to process your payment.
- **BobGo (our shipping platform) and couriers:** your name, address and phone number, so your order can be booked and delivered. Couriers used are The Courier Guy, and Fastway or RAM where they are quicker or cheaper for your address.
- **Google (Gmail):** to send order and service emails and to receive your messages.
- **Vercel (website hosting) and Supabase (database):** to run the website and store your order and contact information. Our database is hosted in Ireland (European Union).

We do not sell your personal information.

## 5. Storage outside South Africa

Our hosting, database and email providers may store your information on servers outside South Africa. These providers are bound by laws, binding rules or agreements that provide adequate protection for your personal information.

## 6. Cookies and analytics

We use only essential cookies (for example, to keep the admin area secure). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not track you across websites.

## 7. How long we keep it

- **Order records:** 5 years, for tax and accounting purposes.
- **Contact messages:** 2 years after the inquiry is resolved.
- **Newsletter subscriptions:** until you unsubscribe.

## 8. Your rights

You may ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, to correct or delete it, or to object to its processing, and you may withdraw marketing consent at any time. Contact our Information Officer using the details above.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator at [inforegulator.org.za](https://inforegulator.org.za).

## 9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your information, but no system is completely secure.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.
