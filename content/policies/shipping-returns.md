## Delivery

We deliver **nationwide, door to door, to major cities and metropolitan areas**. We can't currently deliver to deep rural areas or outlying postal codes. We don't offer pick-up points or collection.

- **Delivery fee:** a flat rate of R86 per order.
- **Free delivery:** orders over R450 ship free.
- **Couriers:** we ship with The Courier Guy, and may use Fastway or RAM where they are quicker or cheaper for your address.

## Processing and delivery times

We pack your order within 1 business day after payment is confirmed. Courier delivery usually takes 2 to 4 business days. Delivery times are estimates and are not guaranteed.

We will email you a courier name and tracking number when your order ships. You can also check your order on our [order status page](/order-status).

## Returns

Because our products are cosmetics, we cannot accept returns of products that have been opened or used, for hygiene and safety reasons, **unless the product is defective or not as described**.
