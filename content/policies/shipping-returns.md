# Shipping & Returns

Last updated: 18 November 2026

## 1. Delivery

We deliver **nationwide, door to door**. We don't offer pick-up points or collection.

- **Delivery fee:** R87 per order to major cities and metropolitan areas, and R120 per order to rural and remote areas. Your exact fee is shown at checkout once you enter your postal code.
- **Free delivery:** orders over R450 ship free.
- **Couriers:** we ship with The Courier Guy, and may use Fastway or RAM where they are quicker or cheaper for your address.

## 2. Processing and delivery times

We pack your order within 3 to 5 business days after payment is confirmed. Courier delivery usually takes 2 to 4 business days. Delivery times are estimates and are not guaranteed.

We will email you a courier name and tracking number when your order ships. You can also check your order on our [order status page](/order-status).

## 3. Delivery details and failed deliveries

You are responsible for entering an accurate delivery address and phone number.

If a parcel is returned to us because the courier could not reach you after the standard delivery attempts, or because the address was wrong, you will need to pay a re-delivery fee, or we will deduct the original shipping cost from any refund.

## 4. Change-of-mind returns

You may return a product within **7 days** if you changed your mind. The product must be unopened, unused, with intact tamper seals and in its original packaging.

- You pay the cost of returning it.
- Refunds are issued once the returned parcel arrives back at our premises and passes inspection, confirming that the product is unopened and undamaged.
- The original outbound delivery fee (or the R87 / R120 fee, if free shipping applied) is non-refundable on change-of-mind returns, unless prohibited by law.

Because our products are cosmetics, we cannot accept returns of opened or used products for hygiene and safety reasons, unless they are defective or not as described (see section 6).

## 5. Goods damaged in transit

If your order arrives damaged, report it within **3 business days** after delivery at kuntania.contact@gmail.com.

- Include your order number, a description of the damage and clear photos of the product and packaging.
- We cover the cost of the return, and will arrange a replacement or refund once we have confirmed the issue.

## 6. Defective goods and warranty

Genuinely defective goods (for example, a manufacturing defect) carry a **6-month warranty** from delivery. Under the Consumer Protection Act, you are entitled to goods that are of good quality and fit for purpose.

- Contact us at kuntania.contact@gmail.com with your order number, a description of the defect and clear photos.
- We cover the cost of the return, and will arrange a replacement or refund once we have confirmed the defect.

## 7. Refunds

To receive a refund, you must apply within **7 business days**. Approved refunds are paid back to the original payment method (via Yoco) within **30 days**. Your bank may take additional time to show the refund.

Your rights under the Consumer Protection Act are not affected by this policy.
